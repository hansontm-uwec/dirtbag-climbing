Our Story

We are climbers, skiers, mountaineers and adventures. Dirtbag climbing was founded by people whose love for the mountains is unmatched. We constantly seek to create gear that will survive the harshest conditions, and get you home safe. We create gear for the person who pushes themselves, just to see how far they can go. For the person who risks everything, in order to see if he can find himself. We are commited to that mission, and we will continue to make products forged from the harshest conditions, abd built for those who go there.

CEO/Founder
Harry Penner

Harry's dream was to always build a brand in the climbing industry. Dirtbag Climbing was founded because of that dream, and his insatiable love for the mountains. Being a highly skilled and experienced ice climber and mountaineer he brought his significant experience to bear when developing the products for dirtbag climbing.
