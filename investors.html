Careers

If you are interested in working at Dirtbag Climbing please drop us an email. We are a group of highly motivated and skilled climbers who share a passion for the outdoors, and most importantly climbing and skiing. When you work at Dirtbag Climbing you will notice that we value our people as our number one priority, you will be treated like family, and will be given responsibility to do your work how you want to do it.

Not only are we dedicated employees to the company, and our mission, we are also climbers. If conditions are right we will take an extended lunch break to go out cragging, or take a day off for a powder day. We offer discounted climbing gym memberships, 500 dollars per year for gear purchases, and 1,500 dollars after 18 months for bucket list adventure trips. We offer all of this along with competitive salary, and fully matched 401k contrituions up to 100% of your contribution limit.

If you are interested please send us an email with your resume and cover letter to careers@dirtbagclimbing.com
